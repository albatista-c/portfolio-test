Hello, I'm Anna!

I'm a master's student in Human Centered Design and Engineering at the University of Washington in Seattle. I'm interested in user experience design, human-computer interaction, and creating digital experiences that are accessible, intuitive, and enjoyable.

My academic background combines linguistics, data science, and technology. Through these fields, I've developed an appreciation for how people communicate, how they interact with complex systems, and how research and data can inform better design decisions.

I enjoy approaching challenges as a problem-solver: understanding people's needs, asking thoughtful questions, and translating research and ideas into practical, human-centered solutions.

My Background

Before pursuing graduate study in HCDE, I studied linguistics and data science at the University of Washington. My research experiences have included computational modeling, language technology, and interdisciplinary scientific research.

I've also worked on educational and language-focused projects, including developing materials for a Kazakh language textbook and exploring ways technology can support language learning. These experiences strengthened my interest in making information more understandable and technology more useful to the people who rely on it.

I'm now building on this interdisciplinary experience to pursue a career in UX design and research, with a focus on responsible, inclusive, and evidence-based design.

What I'm Interested In

I'm especially interested in the intersection of people, technology, communication, and design. I want to help create products that address real needs while making interactions feel clear, thoughtful, and rewarding.

Beyond the Screen

Outside of my academic and professional interests, I'm involved in cycling and fitness. As a group fitness instructor and former president of the UW Husky Cycling Club, I've enjoyed bringing people together around shared interests and helping create welcoming, engaging experiences.

These experiences have reinforced my appreciation for community, communication, and designing experiences around the people participating in them.
